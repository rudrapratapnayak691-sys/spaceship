Recovered water is purified and routed to the plant-growth loop. Some water is returned for reuse, while plants provide a supplemental source of fresh food.

01WATER ELECTROLYSIS

Electrical energy drives a process that separates water into hydrogen and oxygen. Hydrogen requires safe handling and storage or further processing.

02CO₂ REMOVAL

Air revitalization equipment captures carbon dioxide from cabin air to help maintain a suitable atmosphere for the crew.

03HEAT REJECTION

Heat exchangers collect excess heat and transfer it to coolant loops. Radiators reject that heat to the surrounding environment.
